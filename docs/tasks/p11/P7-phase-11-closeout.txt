EXCLUSION / PROSPECTIVE EVIDENCE — REAL POSTGRESQL REQUIRED
Prove at minimum this sequence:

1. run/candidate is included and creates/resolves an Article with Category A;
2. operator config changes categorization and a later included observation applies Category B without increasing Article cardinality and remains `unchanged` when Source fields match;
3. operator rule changes to exclusion and the same candidate later produces:
   - one `excluded` observation;
   - NULL Article ID on that exclusion observation;
   - persisted exclusion rule/reason;
   - no Article insert/update/touch/hide/archive;
   - no Article identity lock/lookup path for exclusion;
   - existing Article and current Category membership left intact;
4. later config returns candidate to inclusion and the same Source Article identity resolves normally with then-current Category reconciliation.

This sequence is key proof that Phase 11 edits are prospective and do not redefine Article identity or become retroactive moderation.
