Replace the relational tenant-style `publications` model with a true singleton settings representation. Prefer a clearly named table such as `publication_settings`.

The singleton settings data required now is:
- `name`;
- `active_for_collection`;
- `public_status`;
- timestamps needed by current persistence behavior.

Do not retain a Publication UUID or slug. Do not invent a dynamic tenant identifier solely to satisfy a primary-key habit.

Preserve current name/public-status validation semantics. The existing public statuses remain `private` and `public`.
