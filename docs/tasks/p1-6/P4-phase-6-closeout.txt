## P1 — Local consumer API

Verify:

- ordinary visitor/customer local reads require only local Profile/state/freshness configuration and do not require upstream base URL/bearer credentials;

- `never_synced`, `stale_cutoff`, `disabled`, and `unavailable` expose no renderable retained Article/Profile payload through the normal customer model;
