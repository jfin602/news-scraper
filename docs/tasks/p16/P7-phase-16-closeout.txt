MODEL / REASONING / USAGE
- Recommended configuration: `Sol Light`.
- Model-family basis: closeout is the repository's independent final review boundary, so Sol's stronger base code-reading/review capability is intentionally used to challenge work produced mostly by Luna while Light reasoning keeps the normal review economical.
- Reasoning basis: Light reasoning is sufficient for the explicit three-pass contract: contract/evidence verification, a fresh adversarial failure/edge-case pass, and a separate code-quality/structural pass; deeper reasoning is reserved for an observed blocker that cannot be confidently classified as a bounded closeout defect.
- Complexity / quality floor: `High`.
- Estimated usage: `High`.
- Lower-cost alternative considered: `Luna High`; rejected because repository policy deliberately keeps Sol Light as the independent closeout baseline to increase the chance of catching cross-prompt integration and quality gaps without paying for deep reasoning by default.
- Escalation trigger: any pass exposes material architectural ambiguity, difficult cross-subsystem root cause, unresolved transaction/concurrency ownership, security-boundary uncertainty, or another defect that cannot be confidently repaired inside the bounded governed Phase 16 behavior.
- Escalation target: `Terra Ultra` when the dominant need is deeper reasoning over explicit PostgreSQL/state interactions; use a higher-effort Sol configuration instead if the blocker requires stronger independent cross-system inference or contract reconciliation. Meaningful behavior-preserving structural refactors use the separate `Terra High` refactor handoff below.
- Estimate confidence: `High`.
- Efficiency rationale: Sol Light supplies stronger independent review while the three-pass hardening procedure, bounded-fix rule, explicit escalation trigger, and Terra High refactor handoff prevent low reasoning from papering over a hard defect.

PASS 1 — CONTRACT / EVIDENCE
Validate the final tree against the actual Phase 16 contracts and roadmap exit gate.

A. PERSISTENCE / MIGRATION — LEVEL 4 REQUIRED
Prove against disposable PostgreSQL:
- migration from zero creates the canonical duplicate schema and Collection-run effect fields;
- canonical unordered review pair uniqueness and self/reversed-pair protection;
- bounded candidate state/origin/confidence/evidence/signal integrity;
- at-most-one Duplicate group membership per Article;
- every committed group has one non-null Primary and that Primary is a member of that group;
- Article visibility is not encoded as duplicate role;
- topology operations cannot cascade-delete Articles/observations/provenance;
- duplicate effect counters are nonnegative and do not alter canonical processing-outcome arithmetic.

B. SIGNAL / REVIEW STATE — LEVEL 2 + LEVEL 4 REQUIRED
Prove:
- exact full canonical-identity URL equality across different Sources is strong deterministic evidence;
- normalized-title equality and all other implemented resemblance remain weak review-only evidence;
- same-Source repeats/conflicts remain Article identity rather than duplicate grouping;
- generic/recurring-title and related-coverage cases never auto-group without strong evidence;
- canonical pair ordering, reasons/confidence, and evidence fingerprint are deterministic;
- unchanged evidence keeps dismissed candidates dismissed and does not recreate pending work;
- materially changed evidence is distinguishable and reconsidered only through governed state transition;
- concurrent same-pair detection converges to one review row.

C. PRIMARY SELECTION — LEVEL 2 + LEVEL 4 REQUIRED
Exercise every deterministic criterion independently and in precedence order:
- no trustworthy original-publisher metadata is inferred when the current schema does not contain governed metadata;
- higher numeric Source priority wins under the Phase 16 configured convention;
- metadata completeness/destination quality tie behavior;
- credible parsed/earlier publication time;
- persisted timing fallback;
- stable Article-ID final tie;
- member input/SQL row ordering cannot change the winner;
- Source-priority edits do not launch bulk historical reselection, while legitimate new/merge/reevaluation operations may use current configuration;
- hidden Primary remains valid and is not silently replaced for public convenience.

D. GROUP TOPOLOGY / CONCURRENCY — LEVEL 4 REQUIRED
Prove:
- two ungrouped Articles form one group;
- grouped + ungrouped adds one member;
- repeated same-group evidence is idempotent;
- two existing groups strongly connected merge atomically with every membership retained;
- concurrent same-pair, crossing-pair, and group-merge cases converge without overlapping membership or multiple Primaries;
- deterministic lock order/transaction behavior avoids deadlock or converts only genuinely expected retryable states according to implementation contract;
- injected failures roll back memberships, Primary, candidate disposition, and current candidate Article/observation changes coherently;
- weak/dismissed evidence cannot mutate topology;
- no Article/observation/provenance deletion occurs during merges.

E. COLLECTION COMPOSITION / EFFECTS — LEVEL 3 + LEVEL 4 + LEVEL 5 WHERE FIXTURES APPLY
Prove duplicate evaluation occurs only after included Source-scoped Article identity/persistence and inside the governed atomic candidate composition:
- `created`/`updated`/`unchanged` remain canonical outcomes;
- duplicate effects compose orthogonally and never replace those outcomes;
- strong first-time evidence may produce both review-created and grouped effects;
- weak evidence creates review work without grouping;
- repeated unchanged/dismissed/same-group evidence has exact zero/new-effect behavior;
- Source-filtered, normalization-failed, link-rejected, Relevance-excluded, and pre-identity failures never run duplicate logic;
- duplicate processing failure rolls back the current candidate's Article/observation/category/duplicate state rather than leaving a committed canonical success with missing required effects;
- exact persisted `duplicate_review_created_count` and `duplicate_grouped_count` match committed effects while processing arithmetic remains unchanged;
- existing Source-filter/Relevance/Article identity/run accounting regressions remain green.

F. PUBLIC FEED — LEVEL 3 + LEVEL 4 REQUIRED; LEVEL 6 REGRESSION REQUIRED
Prove:
- visible ungrouped Articles remain eligible;
- visible Primary is eligible and visible non-Primary is suppressed;
- hidden Primary does not cause visible non-Primary resurrection;
- Source/Category filters and literal keyword search cannot resurrect a non-Primary;
- keyset pagination operates over the already-suppressed canonical eligible stream with no duplicate/omitted rows in tie-heavy static data;
- chronological ordering/cursor semantics remain unchanged;
- selected Primary uses its own stored `original_url`/title/Source/date/category data;
- all non-Primary Articles/observations remain stored and queryable outside the ordinary public feed;
- existing root/feed browser loading/themes/responsive/accessibility/external-link/discovery behavior remains green in real Chromium.

PASS 2 — ERROR / EDGE-CASE ADVERSARIAL REVIEW
After Pass 1 is green on a candidate tree, perform a separate fresh review whose goal is to find plausible Phase 16 failures not already guaranteed merely because focused tests are green. Derive hypotheses from actual code and important producers/consumers.

At minimum consider applicable cases such as:
- malformed/self/reversed canonical pairs and invalid candidate/signal/confidence states;
- digest collisions where full canonical URL/title values differ;
- extremely common/generic equal titles producing many weak candidates without accidental strong promotion;
- same-Source external-ID/canonical conflicts that might accidentally escape into duplicate grouping;
- repeated observations of created/updated/unchanged Articles across multiple Sources and endpoint runs;
- stale candidate state between detection and grouping, including a candidate becoming dismissed/merged before mutation;
- two or more concurrent strong relations sharing one Article, crossing groups, or connecting already-merging groups;
- deterministic lock ordering, uniqueness-conflict handling, rollback after partial membership movement, and retry/idempotency behavior;
- group Primary/member integrity during creation, add-member, same-group reevaluation, and two-group merge;
- Source priority ties/changes and every later Primary tie-break, including equal timestamps/metadata and final UUID tie;
- hidden/archived visibility combinations, especially hidden Primary + visible non-Primary;
- category/source/search filter and cursor combinations that might move suppression after pagination or resurrect a member;
- duplicate-effect counters on zero-candidate, all-filtered, rejected, excluded, failure, partial-run, repeated-review, and multi-review cases;
- database failure during the composed Article+duplicate transaction and preservation of earlier candidates in the same run;
- stale/missing Article/Source/group/candidate references and bounded error translation without SQL/internal leakage;
- accidental cascade/deletion of Article observations or Category/Relevance provenance;
- performance-path regressions such as full-table fuzzy/title scanning accidentally introduced despite indexed exact lookup design.

For every plausible hypothesis classify it as:
1. already protected by implementation plus meaningful executed evidence;
2. governed behavior is clear but coverage is weak — add/strengthen a bounded regression test/procedure;
3. concrete bounded Phase 16 defect — repair it and add focused regression coverage where practical;
4. meaningful behavior-preserving refactor — stop and use the Terra High handoff below;
5. material ambiguity/hard defect — stop for model escalation/replanning/correction.

Do not weaken conservative duplicate behavior merely to satisfy a test. When the contract does not determine the right behavior, report ambiguity rather than inventing policy during closeout.

After any bounded change, rerun focused evidence and every broader suite/evidence level invalidated by that change before proceeding.
