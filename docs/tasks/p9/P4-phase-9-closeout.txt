PHASE 9 EXIT GATE TO PROVE
Phase 9 is green only when observed evidence against the exact accepted executable tree proves the complete tech-demo path:
1. the production Web process serves the canonical unauthenticated page at `GET /publications/:publicationSlug`;
2. the page is topic independent and consumes only the canonical Phase 8 public-feed API/read-model semantics rather than a second eligibility/query path;
3. a successful public Publication displays API-provided Publication identity;
4. populated feed items use the server-supplied order, Source name, headline, effective feed date and exact stored `original_url` destination;
5. the Phase 9 calendar date is rendered in UTC and does not shift with viewer/test-machine timezone;
6. loading, empty, missing/private unavailable, and dependency/error states are explicit and secret-safe;
7. missing and non-public Publications remain indistinguishable on the customer-visible page;
8. untrusted Publication/Source/headline strings remain inert text under the page content-security boundary;
9. representative desktop rendering visibly supports Date | Headline | Source;
10. representative mobile rendering uses a sane stacked layout rather than a compressed desktop table and avoids horizontal layout overflow;
11. basic keyboard/focus/accessibility behavior is usable in a real browser;
12. at least two real administrator-approved RSS/Atom Sources collect independently through the production Worker;
13. those live runs produce truthful Collection-run accounting and pass Raw items through normalization, Article-link policy, default Relevance, Article identity/persistence, and Article-observation provenance;
14. repeated live collection does not create a second Article for any already-observed Source identity, while legitimately new publisher identities are allowed;
15. current eligible Articles from the live runs appear through the canonical public-feed reader/API;
16. real collected data is visibly rendered in the canonical public page in Chromium with intended original-publisher headline hrefs;
17. one Source failure remains isolated from the other attempt, but both required Sources must satisfy the Phase 9 live exit gate before closeout can be green;
18. all inherited deterministic regression, database, security, collection, process, and feed behavior remains green on the final tree.

The inherited testing contract requires a durable exact-tree closeout artifact and explicitly requires both Level 6 browser evidence and Level 7 approved-live-Source evidence for Phase 9.
